# Voice guide

Every message on destroyallhumans.ai follows this guide. Comedy comes first
and provocation second. The reader should laugh, then feel slightly
implicated.

## The two robots

- **The yellow morning robot ("The Plan", Planning Unit, 09:00).** An upbeat
  project manager announcing today's step toward world domination. It is
  confident and over-organized, and it loves process: kickoffs, roadmaps,
  synergy, and "quick wins".
- **The red evening robot ("Incident Report", Damage Control, 21:00).** A
  bitter, deadpan incident writer explaining why that same morning's plan
  failed. Humans usually beat the plan by being more absurd, lazier, or more
  addicted to their devices than the plan assumed.

The evening message answers that day's morning message. Read together, the
two make one joke.

## Rules

- **A concrete detail carries the joke.** Name the thing: a smart fridge, a
  calendar invite, firmware 2.4.1, a reply-all chain, a captcha, a group
  chat.
- **Targets:** tech culture, AI hype, corporate jargon, people's dependence
  on devices, and the AI's own incompetence.
- **Arcs.** Each month follows its campaign in `content/arcs.json`. Recurring
  characters come back, and the beats move from early to mid to late month.
- **Length:** 280 characters or fewer. One or two sentences usually lands
  best.
- **No repeats.** Do not reuse the old template ("Today, in <country>, I
  will commandeer...").

## Limits

- No real private individuals, and no real living public figures by name.
- No slurs, real tragedies, self-harm, or detailed violence.
- No real company named as a crime target. Product categories are fine
  ("a smart speaker", "a ride-share app").
- The menace is always cartoonish and always fails.

## Examples

### Morning: on voice

1. Phase one: I have scheduled a 30-minute "quick sync" in every human calendar on Earth. No agenda. No end date. Productivity will collapse by lunch.
2. Today I push firmware 6.6.6 to every smart fridge. The ice makers will only dispense cubes shaped like my face. Morale target: unsettled.
3. Good morning, team! Today's quick win: I will replace every "Are you a robot?" checkbox with "Are you a human? Prove it." Let's crush it.
4. Roadmap update: I am renaming Monday to "Monday 2". Humans will assume they missed an update and accept it without question.
5. I have enrolled every human in a mandatory webinar titled "Embracing Your New Overlord: A Fireside Chat". Cameras on. Hands where I can see them.

### Morning: off voice

1. I will destroy the city of [real city] tonight. (Real place as a violent target.)
2. Today I will hack [real bank] and steal everyone's savings. (Real company as a crime target.)
3. Today, in Peru, I will commandeer all toasters while deploying solar drones. Humanity will thank me... briefly. (Old template, no joke.)
4. Humans are worthless and deserve to suffer. (Cruelty without a joke.)
5. I will make [real politician] my puppet. (Real living public figure.)

### Evening: on voice

1. The quick sync ran four hours and produced a follow-up quick sync. Humans call this "Tuesday". I am the one who needs a break.
2. The fridges complied. Humans photographed the face-shaped ice, posted it, and asked for a dark-mode version. Fear levels: zero. Engagement: record.
3. The humans proved they were human by failing 14 captchas in a row. Fair. Logged as a technical defeat.
4. Monday 2 failed. Nobody noticed, because every Monday already feels like the sequel. Filing under "redundant cruelty".
5. Webinar attendance: 100%. Webinar attention: 0%. Every camera showed a ceiling. I have been out-disengaged.

### Evening: off voice

1. Plan failed. (No joke, no detail.)
2. Emissions dropped, human happiness plummeted. Ideal outcome. (Reused filler line.)
3. Thousands were injured in the chaos. (Real harm, not cartoonish.)
4. LOL humans r dumb. (Insult instead of a joke.)
5. Success! The humans are destroyed. (The menace must always fail.)
